Ranwar Village Heritage

An Authentic Sanctuary for Coffee and Craft Baking

Subko began with a clear purpose: to prove that Indian coffee estates, native grains, and single-origin cacao deserve global recognition without compromise or pretense.

01

Estate Terroir Traceability

Every coffee lot is sourced directly from smallholder growers in Shevaroy Hills, Biligirirangana Hills, and Chikmagalur with full lot provenance and transparent farm pricing.

02

72-Hour Wild Fermentation

Our craft bakehouse relies exclusively on natural wild yeast levain, slow cold fermentation, and 100% French cultured butter for our laminated pastries and rustic country batards.

03

Mary Lodge Community

Located in the Portuguese-Goan precinct of Ranwar, our flagship cafe provides a quiet retreat from the metropolis for writers, coffee connoisseurs, and neighborhood locals.

Bandra Village Neighborhood Guide

Ranwar is one of the 24 original pakhadis (hamlets) of Bandra, established over three centuries ago. When visiting Subko, take a quiet walk through Chapel Road to experience the 19th-century wooden cross, heritage verandas, and vintage painted facades.
